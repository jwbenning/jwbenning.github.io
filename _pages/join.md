_Updated August 2025_  

We're always looking for collaborators. Cornell offers a stimulating intellectual environment nestled amidst the inspiring natural beauty of Upstate New York. 

## Postdocs  
**We're searching for a postdoctoral collaborator.** The project will leverage long-term demographic and environmental data with longitudinal population genomic analyses of _Clarkia xantiana_ to better understand interrelationships between environmental change, evolution, and population demography. The work will largely comprise population genomics and simulation modeling work. You need only motivation to learn, not experience in, these approaches to be successful in this position. Salary will be at [NIH postdoc funding levels](https://www.niaid.nih.gov/grants-contracts/salary-cap-stipends), based on years experience. 20% of your time will be earmarked for personal projects and professional development. Up to three years of funding are available. 

We're also interested in sponsoring candidates for postdoctoral fellowships. Reach out to [John](mailto:jbenning@cornell.edu) if you'd like to explore that option.

## Graduate students  
**We're recruiting PhD students** for the Fall 2025 application period, to begin graduate school in Fall 2026. 

If you are interested in joining the Benning Lab as a PhD student at Cornell University, please use this form to provide some information and describe your motivation for joining the lab. 

Each fall, John will review and respond to all inquiries submitted from this form. All inquires submitted before Sept 1st will be reviewed and responded to in the first week of September, all inquiries sent between Sept 2nd and Oct 1st will be reviewed and responded to in the first week of October and all inquires sent between Oct 2nd and Nov 1st will be reviewed and responded to the first week of November.

If for any reason you do not receive a response in the timeline described, or you have questions that require answers on different timelines (e.g., you want to apply for funding opportunities with deadlines OR you have questions about eligibility), feel free to reach out directly to [John](mailto:jbenning@cornell.edu).

Read more about Cornell’s application process [here](https://ecologyandevolution.cornell.edu/graduate). And you can compare stipends among similar graduate programs with this [great resource](https://rhettrautsaw.app/shiny/BiologyPhDStipends/) created by Michelle L. Gaynor and Rhett M. Rautsaw.
