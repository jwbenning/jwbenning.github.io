We're always looking for collaborators. Cornell offers a thriving intellectual environment set among inspiring natural beauty. 

_Updated August 2025_

## Postdocs  
We're searching for a postdoctoral collaborator. This will be a combination of genomic and modeling work, but if you want to do field work there will be many opportunities.

We're also interested in sponsoring candidates for postdoctoral fellowships. Reach out if you'd like to explore that option.

## Graduate students  
We're recruiting PhD students for the Fall 2025 application period, to begin graduate school in Fall 2026. 

If you are interested in joining the Benning Lab as Ph.D. student at Cornell University, please use this form to provide some information and describe your interests and motivation for joining the lab. 

Each fall, Benning will review and respond to all inquiries submitted from this form. All inquires submitted before Sept 1st will be reviewed and responded to in the first week of September, all inquiries sent between Sept 2nd and Oct 1st will be reviewed and responded to in the first week of October and all inquires sent between Oct 2nd and Nov 1st will be reviewed and responded to the first week of November.
If for any reason you do not receive a response in the timeline described, or you have questions that require answers on different timelines (e.g., you want to apply for funding opportunities with deadlines OR you have questions about eligibility), feel free to reach out directly to Dr. Womack with those questions (email link at bottom of page).

Read more about Cornell’s application process [here](https://ecologyandevolution.cornell.edu/graduate). And you can compare stipends among similar graduate programs with this [great resource](https://rhettrautsaw.app/shiny/BiologyPhDStipends/) created by Michelle L. Gaynor and Rhett M. Rautsaw.
