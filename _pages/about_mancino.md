**Ella Mancino**  
Research Specialist (jointly with the Agrawal Lab)  
[emm359@cornell.edu](mailto:emm359@cornell.edu)

Ella received her B.S. from Cornell University in May 2026, where she conducted her honors thesis in the Agrawal lab focused on the impacts of pollination and herbivory on fitness of the greater fringed gentian (*Gentianopsis crinita*). Ella is a botanist and plant ecologist intrigued by fundamental questions in ecology and evolutionary biology: How does herbivory mediate sympatric speciation? What maintains species boundaries among overlapping conspecifics? How do biotic interactions maintain species range limits? Ella continues to study the greater fringed gentian, investigating how herbivory may both directly and indirectly impact the fitness of *G. crinita*, leading to local adaptation. Within this same system, Ella is also working to understand the demography and population genetics of *G. crinita* and to resolve phylogenetic relationships within *Gentianopsis*. In her free time, Ella enjoys ultra-marathon running and vegan baking!
