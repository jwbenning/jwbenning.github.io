Maddie is an evolutionary field biologist who uses genomic variation across space and time to explore local adaptation and species responses to rapid environmental change. She received her undergraduate degree at California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo, a Master's degree at the University of British Columbia with Darren Irwin, and a PhD from Cornell University with Mike Webster. Outside of work, she loves knitting, hiking, and really any excuse to be outdoors.
