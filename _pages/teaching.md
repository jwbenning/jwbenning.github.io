## Mentoring

Most of my teaching happens one person at a time — with graduate students, postdocs,
technicians, and undergraduate researchers in the lab. How I try to do that is written
down on the [principles]({{ '/principles/' | relative_url }}) page, and if you are
thinking about joining, start on the [join]({{ '/join/' | relative_url }}) page.
