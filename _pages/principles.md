<p class="prin-lede">
  These are the commitments that shape how we do science and run the lab.
</p>

<div class="prin-list">

  <div class="prin">

    <div class="body">
      <h2>Everyone can be a scientist</h2>
      <p>Curiosity, not credentials, makes a scientist. We welcome people from every background and stage, and we build a lab where asking questions and learning by doing are open to all.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="prin">
