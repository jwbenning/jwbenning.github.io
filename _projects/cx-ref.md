We're using trio-binning (long-read sequencing of F1 hybrids together with short-read data from both parents) to build reference-quality, fully phased genome assemblies for the two subspecies of _Clarkia xantiana_. The resulting references will underpin our work on contemporary adaptation, range limits, and the genetic architecture of fitness — and will be a community resource for the broader _Clarkia_ research community.

_With Jacob Landis (Cornell)._
