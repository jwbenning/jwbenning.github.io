CONGENER (*Community Network Genomics of Ecosystem Resilience*) is a ~13-PI collaboration spanning Cornell, UCSC, UCI, and UC Berkeley. The project builds a predictive framework integrating within-species genomic variation, population demography, and species interaction networks to forecast how communities resist, recover, or collapse under perturbation. Our lab leads the Pillar III experimental meadow gardens — 24 one-acre plots at Cornell crossing species richness with within-species genetic diversity, with a planned perturbation experiment and pool-seq time series.

*A Simons Foundation Collaboration in Ecology and Evolution. Founding PIs: Erik Enbody (lead), Carl Boettiger, Chelsea Specht, Courtney Davis, Daniel Anstett, Hannah Marx, Ian Wang, Laura Melissa Guzman, Maria Rebolleda-Gomez, Philipp Messer, Russell Corbett-Detig, Xiangtao Xu, and JWB.*
