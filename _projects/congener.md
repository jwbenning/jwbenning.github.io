CONGENER (*Community Network Genomics of Ecosystem Resilience*) is a ~13-PI collaboration spanning Cornell, UCSC, UCI, and UC Berkeley, linking within-species genomic variation to ecosystem resilience. Our lab leads the experimental meadow-garden pillar at Cornell: 24 one-acre plots crossing species richness with within-species genetic diversity, with a planned perturbation experiment and pool-seq time series.
