Common tansy (*Tanacetum vulgare*) is widespread across its European native range and an aggressive invader in North America. In a common-garden experiment paired with population genomics, we're asking how the species has evolved during invasion — what traits have shifted, whether shifts are driven by adaptation versus founding-population effects, and how genomic variation in the invasive range compares to the native range.

*With Ryan Briscoe Runquist and Dave Moeller (Minnesota).*
