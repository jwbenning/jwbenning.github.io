We pair roughly two decades of demographic and environmental data across dozens of *Clarkia xantiana* populations with a new population-by-year genomic time series recovered from archived seed. The question: as precipitation grows more volatile, are populations actually adapting in time — and can genomic signatures of adaptation predict which populations persist? The work builds on newly assembled [reference genomes](../cx-ref/).

*With Dave Moeller (Minnesota), Monica Geber (Cornell), and Jacob Landis (Cornell).*
