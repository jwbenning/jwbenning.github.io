We're using pool-seq across ~100 populations of _Clarkia xantiana_ — sampled both historically and contemporaneously — to ask how allele frequencies have shifted over recent decades of climate change, and whether the same loci respond to climate across populations and across time. The work leverages long-term demographic and herbarium records from the Moeller and Geber labs, together with newly assembled reference genomes (see [Clarkia reference genomes](../cx-ref/)).

_With Dave Moeller (Minnesota), Monica Geber (Cornell), and Jacob Landis (Cornell)._
