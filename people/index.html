John Benning

Principal Investigator
jbenning@cornell.edu

johnbenning.net

John received his undergraduate degree in Anthropology from UNC-Chapel Hill, then spent a couple years traveling the country doing field biology before starting his PhD at the University of Minnesota with Dave Moeller. He is an evolutionary ecologist broadly interested in the evolutionary processes that mediate ecological patterns. John just moved to Ithaca from Minneapolis with his wife, Anna, and their children, Theo and Torin. When he’s not chasing the kids down the block, John likes to grow plants, attempt various DIY projects, and play drums.
