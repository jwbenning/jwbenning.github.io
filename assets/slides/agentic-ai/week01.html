Pre-course survey · recurring themes

What worries you

That efficiency is not what several steps of the research process are for, and that wide adoption will bring pressure to do everything faster anyway.

Accountability. Whether you will be able to check and validate what comes back.

Running analyses you do not understand, and code you could not read.

Overuse in writing, overreliance in analysis, and people who believe an agent can stand in for a student.

Losing your own skill at the thing. And the energy it costs.

How John uses aAI (agentic AI) · 1 of 3

My basic setup

  • Claude Code in a terminal, not a chat window: it reads and writes my files, runs shell and R, and reaches Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Slack and Chrome.
  • Memory is plain text files, one set per project: CLAUDE.md, TODO.md, handoff.md, session-log.md. A hook injects the last handoff when a session opens; a close-out routine writes the next one.
    Getting memory right will change your aAI experience substantially.
  • Config lives in Drive, symlinked onto three Macs, so office, home and travel are the same machine.
How John uses aAI · 2 of 3

Three example projects

Fringed gentian · coordination

  • Range-wide leaf collection by volunteers — naturalists, preserve managers, agency botanists. 56 populations, 20 states and provinces, no field crew, a three-week flowering window per site.
  • 687 emails across 195 threads, 317 of them sent by us.
  • The agent keeps the tracker, the permit ledger by jurisdiction, and helps draft emails. Nothing is sent without human eyes on it.

Finger Lakes vegetation · exploratory analysis

  • 181 plots surveyed around 1980. The data sheets survived; the location maps did not.
  • The agent wrote the geospatial pipeline that turns one line of text — quadrangle, slope, aspect, elevation — into a candidate polygon on a map.
  • It also found sampling confounds and small data errors.

Clarkia crosstypes · making analyses more robust

  • Building robustness checks for a model whose structure is genuinely labile.
  • Power simulations that let us say more concretely whether an absence of an effect is likely real or a power issue.
  • An audit of the processed data handed over from others against the raw sheets, all 19,956 rows: three disagreements, one substantive.
How John uses aAI · 3 of 3

Some general insights

  • Use aAI to automate tedious tasks.
  • Build in lots of checks, internal and external.
  • Exploratory analyses outside your realm of expertise are OK, but don't fool yourself into complacency.
  • HTML reports are great.
  • Use aAI to do better science, not necessarily more science, and to learn more, not less.
  • Persistent memory changes everything.

For next week · agentic coding and data analysis

What should we run?

Non-linear gradients

Recent theory on species range limits suggests that non-linear environmental gradients may be especially important for limiting distributions.

Skeleton prompt

For 20 well-sampled North American plant species, pull occurrence records from GBIF and climate from WorldClim. For each species define the range core and the range margin, and compare the local spatial rate of change in a set of putatively important climatic variables between them. Give me robustness checks and do not chase results.

Reproduce a published result

An option with an answer key. The archive ships the SLiM model, the parameter files, the R code and the simulation output the paper was built from.

Skeleton prompt

The archive for Benning et al. 2024 (Ecol. Lett. 27:e14472) is public at doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.c.6981030. Run the model at the paper's focal parameters and reproduce the front-versus-core difference in dispersal phenotype on a flat landscape. Do not quit until you can exactly reproduce the paper's results.

A dataset with something wrong in it

A file built on one of my own field experiments — 160 mesocosms at a Clarkia range edge, soil source crossed with precipitation (Benning & Moeller 2021, Ecology). We plant faults in it and do not say what. The published result is the answer key.

Skeleton prompt

Attached is a cleaned dataset from a factorial field experiment, and its data dictionary. Does soil environment affect lifetime fitness? Fit an appropriate model and tell me whether the effect is significant.
