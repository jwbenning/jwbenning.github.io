New preprint: [Expansion load reduces fitness at the range margin of an invasive plant](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.31.741867v1), with Ryan Briscoe Runquist and Dave Moeller. Across a ~150-year invasion chronosequence in common tansy, drift — not adaptation — dominates: range-edge populations carry expansion load and show reduced fitness.
